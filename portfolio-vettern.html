Vettern 🎓

A platform that helps Nigerian companies discover and vet early-career talent through structured, AI-assisted assessments instead of relying on CVs alone. Talent validation, not a job board - live in Nigeria.

Candidates move through a 5-stage vetting pipeline - profile, portfolio, an AI deep dive into their own projects, a practical challenge, and a final domain-specific AI interview - so companies see proof of skill, not just a résumé.

Django React + Vite PostgreSQL Groq AI Resend Cloudinary Render

Project Overview

Companies get hundreds of applications per role, and most CVs look the same on paper - so recruiters filter people out, not because they lack skill, but because a résumé couldn't prove it. Vettern flips that: candidates build a profile, verify their skills, and complete real assessments before they ever speak to a recruiter. Companies then browse people who've already demonstrated their ability, not just listed it.

The 5-Stage Vetting Pipeline

Instead of one generic coding test, candidates climb five stages - each harder to fake than the last. The standout is Stage 3, the Project Deep Dive: AI-generated questions pulled from the candidate's own submitted projects, not a question bank. "Why Django over Flask here?" is a lot harder to bluff than a multiple-choice quiz.

The Business Model

Free for students, always. Companies pay 10% of the intern's monthly stipend, off-platform, only once they take someone on.

The Vetting Pipeline, Stage by Stage

1

Profile

Skills, education, and experience - the foundation everything else builds on.

2

Portfolio Submission

GitHub, projects, and certifications - reviewed by AI, with an identity check and full attempt history.

3

Project Deep Dive

Fullscreen, screen-monitored - AI asks questions generated from the candidate's own projects to verify they built what they claim. Results emailed via Resend.

4

Practical Challenge

A real task tied to the candidate's role, scored with AI feedback.

5

Domain-Specific Interview

A final AI interview specific to the candidate's domain - the last checkpoint before they are verified.
